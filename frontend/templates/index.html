The Problem
Back-Office Finance is Stuck in the Past
Finance teams spend an overwhelming portion of their workday manually processing invoices — a repetitive, error-prone process that delays payments and frustrates vendors.
  • 🕒
    Time-Consuming Manual Entry

    Each invoice requires manual data entry into ERP systems, consuming 15–20 minutes per document with high error rates.

  • ❌
    Error-Prone OCR Without Context

    Traditional OCR extracts text but cannot understand context — leading to wrong field mapping and failed validations.

  • ⚠️
    Missing Validation Logic

    Duplicate invoices, incorrect GST rates, and amount discrepancies go undetected until payment stage — causing financial risk.

  • 📊
    Zero Real-Time Visibility

    Finance officers lack real-time spend dashboards, making budget tracking and anomaly detection reactive rather than proactive.
